## Principles (draft)

1. Describe signals, never people. The badge and card say what was observed, not what the account is.
2. Explainability first. Every badge above "typical" has at least two reasons on the card.
3. Never hide a post.
4. Honest about uncertainty: a distinct hollow state when data is thin, a caveat line on every card.
5. Fit the host. The badge borrows the host font and sits inline with the handle at the host's density.
